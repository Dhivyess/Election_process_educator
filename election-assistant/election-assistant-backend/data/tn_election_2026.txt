SECTION 3: VOTING PROCESS
================================================================================

=== HOW TO VOTE ON POLLING DAY ===
1. Locate your polling booth — use electoralsearch.in, the Voter Helpline App, 
   or SMS "EPIC <your EPIC number>" to 1950
2. Carry at least ONE valid photo ID from the list below
3. Collect a voter slip from the Polling Officer at the booth
4. Enter the voting compartment (one voter at a time, in private)
5. Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate's name and symbol on the EVM
6. A green light + beep sound confirms your vote is recorded
7. The VVPAT (Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail) machine displays a printed slip 
   with your chosen candidate's name and symbol for EXACTLY 7 seconds — this 
   visually confirms your vote before it is automatically stored

=== VALID PHOTO ID DOCUMENTS (Any ONE of these 12) ===
1. EPIC (Voter ID Card) — most preferred
2. Aadhaar Card
3. Passport
4. Driving Licence
5. PAN Card
6. MNREGA Job Card
7. Bank or Post Office Passbook with photograph
8. Smart Card issued by RGI under NPR
9. Service Identity Cards issued to employees by Central/State Govt/PSUs
10. Health Insurance Smart Card issued under RSBY scheme
11. Pension document with photo
12. Official identity cards issued by MPs/MLAs/MLCs

=== WHAT IS AN EVM? ===
EVM stands for Electronic Voting Machine. It consists of:
- Ballot Unit: The machine where you press the button to vote
- Control Unit: Operated by the Polling Officer — activates the ballot unit
- VVPAT: Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail — shows printed slip for 7 seconds 
  confirming your choice

EVMs are standalone machines with no internet/WiFi connectivity, making 
them tamper-proof against remote hacking. Each EVM is assigned to a specific 
constituency and cannot be reprogrammed on-site.

SECTION 4: VOTER REGISTRATION
================================================================================

=== ELIGIBILITY TO VOTE ===
You can vote if:
- You are a citizen of India
- You are 18 years or older on January 1 of the qualifying year
- Your name is on the Electoral Roll of the constituency
- You are not disqualified under any law

You CANNOT vote if:
- You are a non-citizen of India
- You are below 18 years of age on the qualifying date
- You have been declared of unsound mind by a competent court
- You are disqualified under laws relating to corrupt practices

=== FORM 6 — NEW VOTER REGISTRATION ===
Use this form if you are eligible but not yet registered as a voter.

Eligibility:
- Indian citizen
- 18 years or older on January 1 of the registration year
- Ordinary resident of the constituency

Documents required:
- Age proof: Birth certificate, 10th marksheet, passport, or driving licence
- Address proof: Aadhaar card, utility bill, rental agreement, bank passbook
- Recent passport-size photograph (35mm × 45mm)

Apply at: voters.eci.gov.in (online) or submit to your Booth Level Officer (BLO)

=== FORM 7 — OBJECTION / DELETION ===
Use this form to:
- Object to inclusion of a name in the electoral roll
- Apply for deletion of a name (e.g., deceased voter, person moved away)
- Any eligible elector can file a Form 7 objection against another entry

=== FORM 8 — CORRECTION OF ENTRIES ===
Use this form to correct:
- Name (spelling error, changed name after marriage)
- Address (moved within same constituency)
- Photo (old or unclear photo)
- Age/Date of Birth correction
- Transposition from one part to another within same constituency

=== FORM 8A — TRANSPOSITION ===
Use this form if you have moved to a different address within the SAME constituency.
Your entry is shifted to the correct section of the electoral roll.

=== FORM 12D — POSTAL BALLOT APPLICATION ===
Eligible voters who can apply for postal ballot:
- Armed Forces personnel and their spouses
- Persons with Disabilities (PwD) with 40% or more disability
- Essential services voters on polling duty
- Senior citizens aged 85 years or above
- COVID-19 patients or quarantined persons

Apply at least 5 days before polling day.

=== WHAT IS AN EPIC CARD? ===
EPIC = Elector's Photo Identity Card (commonly called "Voter ID card")
Issued by the Election Commission of India.
Contains: Your name, photo, address, date of birth, and unique EPIC number.
Even if you lose your physical EPIC, you can download an e-EPIC (digital 
voter ID) from the ECI portal: voters.eci.gov.in

=== WHAT IS A BLO? ===
BLO = Booth Level Officer
A government official assigned to manage voter rolls at the polling booth level.
Responsibilities: Adding new voters, correcting details, door-to-door verification.
Find your BLO: Call 1950 or visit voterportal.eci.gov.in

=== WHAT IS SIR / SSR? ===
SIR = Systematic Intensive Revision
SSR = Special Summary Revision
Annual process where the Election Commission updates electoral rolls through 
door-to-door verification by BLOs. Usually conducted October–January each year.

- Female Polling Officers present at sensitive booths
- Childcare assistance available at select booths

SECTION 6: VOTER RIGHTS AND MODEL CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================

=== YOUR FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS AS A VOTER ===
1. Right to vote freely and in secret — no one can see your vote
2. Right to be treated with dignity at the polling booth
3. Right to accessible facilities (ramps, assistance for PwD)
4. Right to NOT vote for anyone — a voter can press NOTA (None of the Above)
5. Right to check your name on the electoral roll
6. Right to challenge incorrect voter list entries (via Form 7)
7. Right to vote using alternate ID if EPIC is lost

=== WHAT IS NOTA? ===
NOTA = None of the Above
If you do not wish to vote for any candidate, you can press the NOTA button 
on the EVM (last option on the ballot). Your vote is counted but does not 
benefit any candidate. NOTA was introduced by the Supreme Court of India in 2013.

=== MODEL CODE OF CONDUCT (MCC) ===
The MCC comes into effect from the date of election announcement (March 30, 2026).
What it prohibits:
- Announcement of new government schemes or freebies after MCC is in effect
- Use of government resources (vehicles, buildings) for campaigning
- Display of election material within 200 meters of a polling booth
- Campaigning 48 hours before polling day (campaign silence period)
- Distribution of money, gifts, or alcohol to influence voters

Report MCC violations:
- cVIGIL App: Upload photo/video evidence of MCC violations with GPS location
- The complaint reaches the Flying Squad within 100 minutes
- Call 1950

=== CAMPAIGN SILENCE PERIOD ===
No political campaigning is allowed from 48 hours before polling.
That means: No rallies, no speeches, no distribution of material from 
April 21, 2026 (6:00 PM) until polling ends on April 23, 2026 (6:00 PM).

=== VOTER GRIEVANCES ===
- National Voter Helpline: 1950 (toll-free)
- cVIGIL App: For reporting election violations
- National Grievance Portal: electoralsearch.in
- TN CEO Office: www.elections.tn.gov.in
- Grievances can also be filed at the District Election Officer's office

=== CHECKING CANDIDATE BACKGROUND ===
To check a candidate's criminal background, assets, and liabilities:
- MyNeta: myneta.info
- ADR (Association for Democratic Reforms): adrindia.org
- All candidate affidavits are public and available on the ECI website
- Candidates must disclose criminal cases, income, assets, education in their affidavit

SECTION 10: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: When is the polling date for Tamil Nadu 2026?
A: April 23, 2026 (Wednesday). Polling hours: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Q: When are the results coming?
A: Counting of votes is on May 4, 2026. Results will be declared on the same day.

Q: What was the voter turnout?
A: 85.15% — the highest ever recorded for a Tamil Nadu assembly election.
   The 2021 election had a turnout of 72.7%.

Q: How many voters are there in Tamil Nadu?
A: 5,67,07,380 registered voters (approximately 5.67 crore / 56.7 million).

Q: How many candidates contested?
A: 4,023 candidates across all 234 constituencies.

Q: I turned 18 recently. Can I vote in TN 2026?
A: You must have been 18 years or older on January 1, 2026 (the qualifying date) 
   and completed registration before the electoral roll was finalized.

Q: My name is not on the voter list. What do I do?
A: Contact the 1950 helpline immediately, visit electoralsearch.in to check your 
   status, or visit your BLO. On polling day, you cannot vote if your name is 
   not on the electoral roll.

Q: I lost my Voter ID card. Can I still vote?
A: Yes. You can use any of the 12 alternative photo ID documents listed in 
   Section 3. You can also download an e-EPIC (digital voter ID) from 
   voters.eci.gov.in.

Q: What is NOTA?
A: NOTA = None of the Above. If you do not wish to vote for any candidate, 
   press the NOTA button (last option on the EVM ballot). Your vote is 
   counted but does not benefit any candidate.

Q: Can I vote in Tamil?
A: Yes. All EVM ballot papers, official materials, and the 1950 helpline 
   are available in Tamil. The entire process is bilingual (Tamil + English).

Q: What if I have a disability?
A: You have the right to be accompanied by a companion inside the voting booth. 
   Accessible ramps, wheelchairs, and PwD-friendly facilities are available at 
   all booths. You may also be eligible for postal ballot (40%+ disability).

Q: How do I find my polling booth?
A: Visit electoralsearch.in, use the Voter Helpline App, call 1950, or 
   SMS "EPIC <your EPIC number>" to 1950.

Q: How can I check if my constituency is SC/ST reserved?
A: Refer to Section 8 of this document which lists all 234 constituencies 
   with their category (General / SC / ST). You can also check 
   elections.tn.gov.in for the official list.

Q: What is cVIGIL?
A: cVIGIL is an Election Commission of India mobile app that allows citizens 
   to report Model Code of Conduct (MCC) violations by uploading photo/video 
   evidence with GPS location. The complaint reaches the Flying Squad within 
   100 minutes. Download from Google Play or App Store.

Q: When does the Model Code of Conduct end?
A: The MCC ends when the results are announced — May 4, 2026.

Q: How do I apply for a postal ballot?
A: Submit Form 12D to the Election Commission at least 5 days before polling day. 
   Eligible categories: Armed Forces, PwD (40%+), senior citizens (85+), 
   essential services, COVID patients.

Q: Who cannot vote?
A: Non-citizens, persons under 18 years, persons declared of unsound mind by 
   a court, and persons disqualified under election law.

Q: How many parties are contesting?
A: 124 registered parties, with 4,023 total candidates across all constituencies.
   The two main alliances are the DMK-led SPA and the AIADMK-led Alliance.
   Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK, led by actor Vijay) and Naam Tamilar Katchi 
   (NTK, led by Seeman) are contesting independently in all 234 constituencies.
