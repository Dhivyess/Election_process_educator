=== HOW TO CHECK YOUR VOTER ID STATUS ===
1. Visit the official ECI website: voterportal.eci.gov.in
2. You can also call the Voter Helpline: 1950 (toll-free)
3. Use the Voter Helpline App on Android or iOS
4. Visit your nearest Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) office

=== HOW TO REGISTER AS A NEW VOTER (FORM 6) ===
- Eligibility: Indian citizen, 18 years or above, resident of the constituency
- Documents needed: Age proof (birth certificate, 10th marksheet, or passport), address proof (Aadhaar, utility bill, or bank passbook), recent passport-size photograph
- Apply online at: voters.eci.gov.in or submit Form 6 to your Booth Level Officer (BLO)
- Deadline for registration before TN 2026 elections has already passed; registration remains open for future elections

=== HOW TO CHANGE YOUR ADDRESS (FORM 8) ===
- If you moved within the same constituency: Submit Form 8A
- If you moved to a different constituency: Submit Form 8 to delete from old roll, and Form 6 to register at new address
- Documents: New address proof + existing Voter ID or EPIC number
- Apply at: voters.eci.gov.in

=== WHAT IS AN EPIC CARD? ===
EPIC stands for Elector's Photo Identity Card. It is your Voter ID card issued by the Election Commission of India. It contains your name, photo, address, and a unique EPIC number. It serves as a valid photo identity proof for voting.

=== WHAT IS A BLO? ===
A Booth Level Officer (BLO) is a government official appointed by the Election Commission to manage voter rolls at the polling booth level. They help with: adding new voters, correcting details, removing deceased voters, and verifying residence. You can find your BLO through the 1950 helpline or the ECI portal.

=== WHAT IS SIR (SYSTEMATIC INTENSIVE REVISION)? ===
SIR is a process by which the Election Commission periodically revises and updates the electoral rolls. It involves door-to-door verification by BLOs, addition of newly eligible voters, and removal of duplicate or deceased entries. The Special Summary Revision (SSR) is conducted annually, typically between October and January.

=== WHAT TO BRING ON POLLING DAY ===
You must carry at least one of these valid photo ID documents:
1. EPIC (Voter ID Card) — preferred
2. Aadhaar Card
3. Passport
4. Driving Licence
5. PAN Card
6. MNREGA Job Card
7. Bank/Post Office Passbook with photo
8. Smart Card issued by RGI under NPR
9. Service Identity Cards (government employees)
10. Health Insurance Smart Card (RSBY)

=== ELECTRONIC VOTING MACHINES (EVMs) ===
EVMs are used in all Indian elections. How to vote:
1. Verify your name in the electoral roll at the polling booth
2. Collect a voter slip from the Polling Officer
3. Enter the voting compartment
4. Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate's name and symbol
5. A green light and beep confirms your vote
6. VVPAT (Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail) will show a paper slip with the candidate name and symbol for 7 seconds — this confirms your vote

=== VOTER GRIEVANCES ===
- National Voter Helpline: 1950 (toll-free, available in regional languages)
- cVIGIL App: To report Model Code of Conduct violations with photo/video evidence
- National Grievance Service Portal: electoralsearch.in
- You can also contact the Chief Electoral Officer of Tamil Nadu: www.elections.tn.gov.in

=== FORM TYPES EXPLAINED ===
- Form 6: New voter registration
- Form 6A: Registration for overseas voters (NRIs)
- Form 6B: Registration for Aadhaar linkage
- Form 7: Objection to inclusion / Application for deletion (e.g., deceased voter)
- Form 8: Correction of entries in electoral roll (name, address, photo, age)
- Form 8A: Transposition within constituency (same area address change)

=== WHO CANNOT VOTE? ===
- Non-citizens of India
- Persons below 18 years of age on the qualifying date (January 1 of the registration year)
- Persons of unsound mind as declared by a competent court
- Persons disqualified under any law relating to corrupt practices or offences

=== CANDIDATE INFORMATION ===
To check a candidate's criminal background, assets, and liabilities:
- Visit MyNeta: myneta.info
- Visit ADR (Association for Democratic Reforms): adrindia.org
- Candidate affidavits are publicly available on the ECI website

=== MODEL CODE OF CONDUCT (MCC) ===
The Model Code of Conduct comes into effect from the date of announcement of elections. It governs:
- Political parties and candidates' conduct
- Government machinery — no announcement of new schemes or freebies after MCC is in effect
- Media conduct
- Use of government resources for campaigning (prohibited)
Report MCC violations via the cVIGIL app or call 1950.

=== ABSENTEE VOTERS / POSTAL BALLOTS ===
The following categories can vote via postal ballot:
- Service voters (armed forces, police on election duty)
- Persons with disabilities (PwD) — 40% or more disability
- Essential services voters
- Senior citizens above 85 years
- COVID-19 patients/quarantined persons
Apply with Form 12D at least 5 days before polling.

=== POLLING BOOTH LOCATOR ===
Find your polling booth via:

4. Call 1950

=== COMMON QUESTIONS ===

Q: I turned 18 recently. Can I vote in TN 2026?
A: You must have been 18 years or older on January 1, 2026 (the qualifying date) and have completed registration before the electoral roll was finalized. If you missed registration, you can register for future elections at voters.eci.gov.in.

Q: My name is not on the voter list. What do I do?
A: Contact the 1950 helpline immediately, visit electoralsearch.in to check your status, or visit your BLO. On polling day, you cannot vote if your name is not on the electoral roll.

Q: I lost my Voter ID card. Can I still vote?
A: Yes. You can use any of the 12 alternative photo ID documents listed above. You can also download an e-EPIC (digital voter ID) from the ECI portal.

Q: Can I vote in Tamil? Is there Tamil language support?
A: Yes. All ballot papers, EVM labels, and official election materials are available in Tamil. The 1950 helpline provides support in Tamil. Most official election documents are bilingual (Tamil and English).

Q: What if I have a disability and need help to vote?
A: You have the right to be accompanied by a companion of your choice inside the voting booth. Polling booths must be accessible. PwD voters with 40%+ disability are also eligible for postal ballots.
